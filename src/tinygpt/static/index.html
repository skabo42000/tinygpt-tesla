TinyGPT writes like Tesla

A GPT-style language model I built from the ground up (tokenizer, self-attention, transformer blocks and training loop) and trained on a laptop CPU on The Inventions, Researches and Writings of Nikola Tesla (1894). Give it the start of a sentence and watch it continue, one word piece at a time.

How it was built

  1. Tokenizer: a byte-pair encoding (BPE) tokenizer written from scratch and trained on Tesla's book, the same method GPT-2 and GPT-4 use (512 word pieces).
  2. Self-attention: each token decides which earlier tokens matter to it (query, key, value), with a causal mask so it can never peek at the future.
  3. Transformer blocks: multi-head attention plus feed-forward layers, with residual connections, LayerNorm and dropout, stacked 4 deep.
  4. Training: AdamW with learning-rate warm-up and cosine decay, gradient clipping and weight decay, about 30 minutes on a laptop CPU, checkpoints and resume support.
  5. Testing: automated tests prove, among other things, that the model never sees future tokens and that the fast attention gives the same results as the hand-written version.
